# INCIDENT REPORT ANALYSIS: NIST CSF FRAMEWORK

**Project / Scenario:** Network Security Incident & Phishing Response  
**Author / Security Analyst:** Chris R  
**Framework:** NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF)  

---

## Summary
An intern received a phishing email that required them to click an external link and provide their network credentials . This allowed a malicious actor to gain unauthorized access to the internal network using stolen credentials .

---

## Identify
After being escalated to the IT department, analysts discovered that even though the intern was locked out and claimed they could not access their account, the account was actively being used to access the customer database . Other users also reported missing customer data . The IT department conducted an audit to identify affected devices and determine if other databases were compromised .

---

## Protect
To prevent similar attacks in the future, the IT department implemented Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) to ensure a more secure and verified login method .

---

## Detect
The IT department deployed stricter firewall rules and installed IDS/IPS (Intrusion Detection/Prevention System) software to better monitor and protect against future unauthorized attacks .

---

## Respond
The IT department immediately deactivated the intern's network access . They provided staff training on methods to prevent future phishing attempts, informed affected customers of the network data breach, and contacted law enforcement and local regulatory agencies regarding the incident .

---

## Recover
The IT department restored the customer database from the previous night's full saved backup . Because updates made earlier that morning before the backup schedule were missing, all employees were instructed to manually re-enter those morning updates to bring the database up to date .

---

## Reflections / Notes
* **Key Takeaway:** During this project I have discovered that human error remains one of the primary entry points for threat actors, highlighting why technical controls like MFA, IDS/IPS, and continuous employee security awareness training are critical defense-in-depth measures.
